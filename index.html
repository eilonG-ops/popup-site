The menu

Falafel

Dried chickpeas soaked overnight (never canned), ground with a lot of parsley and cilantro, and fried to order. I'm Israeli with Moroccan roots, and this is the one thing I won't cut corners on.

In the pita

Hummus, tahini, chopped salad, pickles, amba (a tangy pickled-mango sauce) and schug (Yemenite green chili sauce) if you like heat. Build it however you want. I'll only judge a little.

Gazoz

The fizzy drink from early Tel Aviv's soda kiosks: house-made fruit syrup, cold sparkling water, and fresh fruit and herbs right in the glass.

Flavors depend on what's good at the market that week. Expect a few, and expect to try more than one.
